Straight answers

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name, and once the project is paid you get the site files. If you ever leave, I hand everything over.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. Monthly care is optional. Without it you can host the site yourself, and I'll show you how.

Can you guarantee I'll rank first on Google?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who promises that. What I can do is fix the things that hold most local businesses back, then show you what changed.

Will you write fake reviews?

Never. Fake or bought reviews break Google's rules and can get a listing suspended. The review cards make it easy for real customers to leave real reviews.

How long does it take?

Usually about a week from the day I have your photos, menu or service list, and details. The slowest part is almost always gathering content.

Who will I be dealing with?

Me, Ethan. No account managers or hand-offs. You call or text the person doing the work.
